Get started with ChitChat!

ChitChat is a complete messaging platform built to make private conversations simple, flexible, and secure. You can use it directly from the web or download the application and stay connected with your people from anywhere.

To get started, create your account and complete your basic profile. You will be asked to provide your full name, username, email address, mobile number, date of birth, bio/about, and other profile details that help build your identity on ChitChat.

Find people and start chatting

After logging in, you can find other ChitChat users using their username, email address, or name. Select the right account, connect with them, and start your conversation.

  • Username/Profile: Use username, profiles or search by name to connect with your friends, family, relatives or network more easily.
  • Email Contacts: Sync your saved email contacts and discover people you already know without searching for every account manually.
  • Mobile Contacts: Use your phone contacts to find people and connect with your existing network more easily.
  • CSV Import (coming soon!): Already have contact data in a CSV file? You can use that data to help import and manage your contacts.

Chat, share and stay connected

ChitChat is designed around an experience similar to modern messaging apps. You can have conversations, send messages, and share different kinds of content seamlessly.

  • Messaging: Send and receive messages smoothly and continue your conversations whenever you want.
  • Media Sharing: Share photos, videos, songs, documents, PDFs, text and other supported media files inside conversations.
  • Document Sharing: Send files and documents directly through chats while keeping conversations and shared content together.
  • Rich Link Previews: Automatically show a compact preview with a thumbnail when sharing PDFs, Instagram posts, YouTube videos, website links, reels and other supported content.
  • Voice Typing: Type messages using your voice and send them instantly without typing anything manually.
  • Voice Instant Messaging: Record your voice directly in the chat and send voice messages instantly, just like WhatsApp.

Manage your conversations

Keep control over your conversations with features such as:

  • Block contacts
  • Archive chats
  • Hide chats
  • Lock chats
  • Delete conversations
  • Recover deleted conversations
  • Daily backups
  • Disappearing Messages
  • Custom Message Timer

The goal is simple: keep improving ChitChat around the things users actually need.
