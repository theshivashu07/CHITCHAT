Get started with ChitChat!

ChitChat is a complete messaging platform built to make private conversations simple, flexible, and secure. You can use it directly from the web or download the application and stay connected with your people from anywhere.

To get started, create your account and complete your basic profile. You will be asked to provide your full name, username, email address, mobile number, date of birth, bio/about, and other profile details that help build your identity on ChitChat.

Create Account Login

Simple and flexible access

You can access ChitChat in whichever way is convenient for you. Use the web application directly or download the application for your device.

Login your way

Sign in normally with your credentials or use available OAuth2-based social authentication options. Once authenticated, your account and conversations remain available through your ChitChat account.

Find people and start chatting

After logging in, you can find other ChitChat users using their username, email address, or name. Select the right account, connect with them, and start your conversation.

Email Contacts

Sync your saved email contacts and discover people you already know without searching for every account manually.

Mobile Contacts

Use your phone contacts to find people and connect with your existing network more easily.

CSV Import

Already have contact data in a CSV file? You can use that data to help import and manage your contacts.

Chat, share and stay connected

ChitChat is designed around an experience similar to modern messaging apps. You can have conversations, send messages, and share different kinds of content seamlessly.

Messaging

Send and receive messages smoothly and continue your conversations whenever you want.

Media Sharing

Share photos, videos, songs, documents, PDFs, text and other supported media files inside conversations.

Document Sharing

Send files and documents directly through chats while keeping conversations and shared content together.

Manage your conversations

Keep control over your conversations with features such as:

  • Block contacts
  • Archive chats
  • Hide chats
  • Lock chats
  • Delete conversations
  • Recover deleted conversations
  • Daily backups

Your profile, your identity

Your ChitChat account starts with a proper profile so people can recognize and connect with you easily.

Full Name Username Email Mobile Date of Birth Bio / About

Additional profile information can be added as the platform grows and new features are introduced.

ChitChat keeps evolving

Team-ChitChat regularly listens to user suggestions and collects feedback from the community. Suggested features can be reviewed and ranked through votes, helping us decide what should be added next.

The goal is simple: keep improving ChitChat around the things users actually need.

Ready to connect?

Join ChitChat, find your people, start a conversation and enjoy a complete messaging experience.
